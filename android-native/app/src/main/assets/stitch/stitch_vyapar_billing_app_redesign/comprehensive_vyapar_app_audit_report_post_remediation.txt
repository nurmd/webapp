### 3. Accessibility & Usability (WCAG 2.1 Level AA) 🔵 ➔ ✅ RESOLVED

#### Issue 3.1: Color Contrast on Amber / Pending Badges (WCAG 1.4.3)
- **Previous State:** Light amber/yellow chips ("Due in 3d", "Verification Pending", "Popular" language badges) had a low contrast ratio of ~3.2:1 against light backgrounds.
- **Verification Post-Fix:**
  - Amber badges updated to Amber 900 (`#78350F`) text on Amber 100 (`#FEF3C7`) background $\rightarrow$ **7.4:1 contrast ratio** (far exceeding the 4.5:1 threshold).
  - Overdue badges updated to Red 800 (`#991B1B`) on Red 100 (`#FEE2E2`) $\rightarrow$ **6.8:1 contrast ratio**.
- **Audit Outcome:** **PASS (WCAG AA Compliant)**.

#### Issue 3.2: Touch Target Sizing on List Micro-Actions (WCAG 2.5.5 / 2.5.8)
- **Previous State:** WhatsApp chat icons, call buttons, and edit pencil triggers had touch envelopes of ~32×32px, creating accidental click hazards near the primary "Pay Now" / "Select" triggers.
- **Verification Post-Fix:** Micro-actions on `{{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_82}}`, `{{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_98}}`, and `{{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_86}}` now incorporate a minimum touch bounding box of **44×44px** with generous visual padding.
- **Audit Outcome:** **PASS**.

#### Issue 3.3: Form & Search Bar Accessible Naming (WCAG 1.3.1, 4.1.2)
- **Previous State:** Input fields relied only on placeholder text without programmatic labels.
- **Verification Post-Fix:** All search inputs now contain explicit `aria-label` attributes (e.g. `aria-label="Search suppliers by name, GSTIN, or phone"` and `aria-label="Search party by name, phone, GSTIN, or city"`).
- **Audit Outcome:** **PASS**.
